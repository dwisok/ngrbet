PANEL 1 — THE IDEA (first sketch, kept it)

A CASINO WITH THE LOSING REMOVED NEVER DRAWN

Knockoff Casino is a 2D casino where the only possible outcome is winning. Every machine, every round, every time. There is no bankroll to protect because there is nothing to bet: you never put anything in, you only take things out.

It runs on ₭ Company Scrip, a currency printed by the page itself. It is worth exactly nothing, which is why we can hand it out without limit.

PANEL 2 — FACTORY 7 mouse is not paid either

WHERE THE MACHINES COME FROM

Everything on this floor was assembled in Factory 7, a badly lit production line staffed by cracked doll heads on a conveyor belt. Each head gets stamped WIN before shipping. Quality control is a demonic mouse that produces electricity when angry. It is always angry.

The lore is a nod to the trashiest, funniest slot games out there: sweatshop satire, cheap toys, big stickers, absurd multipliers. We kept the vibe and threw away the part where you lose.

PANEL 3 — HOW A ROUND PAYS

THE MATH (THERE IS SOME)

Each round, the floor manager advances you a free shift credit of ₭ 100. The machine picks a multiplier, always greater than one, and pays ₭ 100 × multiplier straight into your balance. That's it. No stake ever leaves you, because you never had a stake.

  • WIN! up to ×10
  • BLAM! ×10 to ×30
  • KA-CHING!! ×30 to ×80
  • HOLY MOLY!!! ×80 and beyond

Your balance is saved in your browser, so it keeps growing between visits. Nobody will ever ask for it back.

PANEL 4 — THE SECRET don't tell HR

SPELL M-A-K-S

After a win, there is a 45% chance a letter drops into the collector at the top of the page, in order: M, then A, then K, then S. A HOLY MOLY win always drops one.

Complete the word and the page enters GOD MODE: the screen flashes, everything shakes, and ₭ 1,000,000 is drawn directly into your balance. Then the letters reset and you can do it again. Management has filed a complaint.

PANEL 7 — WHY IT LOOKS LIKE THIS lines wobble on purpose

STILL A DRAFT. ALWAYS WILL BE.

The whole site is drawn live in white ink on a black page. Every line is stroked twice with a small offset that changes nine times a second, so nothing ever sits still — the same "boiling" you see in hand-drawn animation. Boxes, buttons and panels run through a noise filter so no edge is straight.

The margin notes, the coffee ring, the DRAFT stamp, the crossed-out words: all deliberate. A casino that never lets you lose has no reason to look finished.
